Camera test

What your iPhone lets History Scout — a website — do with its camera. This page changes nothing in History Scout and saves nothing. It only asks the camera, and shows you the answers.

Before you start: open this page in Safari, go outside or near a window, and point the phone at something with detail — a sign, a building, a tree line. The test takes about half a minute and switches between the phone's lenses on its own.
