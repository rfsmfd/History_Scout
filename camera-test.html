Camera test

What your iPhone lets History Scout — a website — do with its camera. This page changes nothing in History Scout and saves nothing. It only asks the camera, and shows you the answers.

Before you start: go outside or near a window and point the phone at something with detail — a sign, a building, a tree line. Allow the camera when Safari asks. The test takes about half a minute and switches between the phone's lenses on its own; its progress shows right below.
