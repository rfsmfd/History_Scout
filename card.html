History Scout Field Card

The whole route on one page: from the photograph in the field to the record in ArcGIS. The Owner's Manual has the detail; this is what you carry.

BUILD 34 iPhone · home screen PC · Edge or Chrome
Open History Scout rfsmfd.github.io/History_Scout →

01Photograph itOn the phone · in the field

  1. 1Photograph with the iPhone's Camera app, then tap 🖼 From Library.Full size, with each photo's own location and time. Select many at once; a photo already in History Scout is skipped. For a quick 12 MP shot, 📷 Capture Site.
  2. 2Nothing to answer — take the next one.A new place goes into 📥 Unsorted. Later, open it, tap what it is and ✅ Sort it.
  3. 3Keep photographing.Anything within 300 yards joins the site you're on. The bar at the top says where photos are landing — ＋ New starts a fresh site.
  4. 4Before walking off: 🔍 Check sharpness.Opens the photo at full detail. If it's soft, 🗑 Delete and retake — two taps, and the retake joins the same site.

02The line under the barOn the phone · any time

One line, near the top of the Capture screen, tells you where your work actually is.

☁ Records backed upEverything typed is in your account.
⏳ 3 changes waitingNo signal. They go up by themselves when you have some. Nothing is lost.
· 12 photos to SendPhotos still to go up. They go by themselves; Send pushes them now.
⚠ Not backed upYou're working without an account. Sign in under More to change that.

03Send the photosOn the phone · by itself

  1. 1Photos go to your account on their own.After every save, when the app opens, and when signal returns. There is nothing to remember.
  2. 2More → 📤 Send N photos when you want to push them now.The button is still there. Leave History Scout open until it finishes — iPhones stop web apps sending in the background. Stop and resume any time.
  3. 3Every photo carries a mark.☁ in your account · ⚠ on this phone only · ✖ picture lost.
  4. 4If Send stops, it tells you why.It names the photo, the step it was on and the reason. It does not sit there looking frozen.
Nothing leaves the phone

Sending makes a copy; it never removes anything from the phone. A photo counts as sent only when Google confirms it at exactly the right size.

04Write it up, then file itOn the PC · Edge or Chrome

  1. 1Open the same address and sign in.Once per computer. The list is on the left, the record on the right.
  2. 2Fill in the form with a keyboard.Changes reach the phone in seconds. Only fields you touch are saved.
  3. 3Filing happens by itself while the app is open here.Shortly after you open it, every five minutes, and soon after photos arrive from the phone. Photos into Photos\Category\Site\, lists into Records\. It never overwrites a file and files only what's new.
  4. 4The buttons still work: More → Your computer → 📥 Bring in new.Use it when you don't want to wait. Safari and Firefox will not file — on a computer, use Edge or Chrome.

05Give it the photo you fixedOn the PC · after Photoshop

  1. 1Save the edit beside the original, named after it.<name> edited.png — or just <Site> edited.png when that site has one photo. A second pass is … edited 2.png.
  2. 2It gets picked up on its own, or press ✨ Check for edited photos.An edit saved less than a minute ago waits for the next round — Photoshop may still be writing it. Nothing on the drive is renamed, moved or replaced.
  3. 3The edit also goes up to your account.An edit made here on the PC reaches the account too, not just this computer.
  4. 4From then on the record shows your version.Thumbnails, stamps and exports use it; 🖼 Show original in the viewer compares the two. Both are kept for good.

06Into ArcGISOn the PC · ArcGIS Pro

  1. 1Open History Scout on the PC and let it file.That writes the current list of sites, which the tool needs to see what has changed.
  2. 2In ArcGIS Pro: Catalog → Toolboxes → right-click → Add Toolbox → Tools\History Scout.pyt.Once. After that the toolbox is simply there.
  3. 3Open Bring in History Scout, tick Dry run, press Run.Dry run changes nothing. Read what it says it would do.
  4. 4Untick Dry run and Run again.The boxes come filled in, so there is nothing to type.
What a run does

A field you changed in ArcGIS yourself is kept and reported, never overwritten.
Nothing is ever deleted — a site removed on the phone is only marked.
Every photograph goes in, the original and every edited version.
The newest edited photo becomes the main photo for that place.
Drag a pin off the road onto the building and it stays where you put it.

07Credit and permissionEvery photograph · everywhere

  1. 1Every photograph carries who took it, where it came from, and May publish.Not every picture in the collection is yours — some came from the Library of Virginia and others.
  2. 2Your own work is credited Buddy Faison Photography.Someone else's picture gets a credit line for its source instead.
  3. 3Nothing goes public unless May publish says Yes.Anything not plainly yours starts at Not sure yet.
If you delete something by mistake

More → 🗑 Deleted items and tap Restore. Sites, photos, anything — it comes back on every device. Nothing is ever erased.

Before you remove the home-screen icon

Removing it wipes that phone's copy of everything. Records are safe in your account; a photo is only safe once it shows ☁. Check the marks first, every time.
